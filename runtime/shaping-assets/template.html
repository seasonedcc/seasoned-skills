Why this matters

The framing

The problem this project sets out to solve, and how we will know it is solved — told in the words of the people who live it.

The struggling moment

Tell the story of one real moment where someone got stuck: who they are, what they were trying to get done, what they reached for, and what it cost them. Name the day, the shift, the order — the concrete details are what make people say “yes, that is exactly it” or “no, that is not how it works here.”

Today's way, and where it breaks

Describe how the work gets done right now, end to end, including the parts that happen outside any system. Then say precisely where it breaks, and what people do to cope when it does.

The outcome test

What has to be true afterwards for this to have worked. Write it so anyone can check it without asking us — an observable difference in someone's day, not a feature list.

Why now

What changed to make this worth doing this month rather than next year. Growth, a deadline, a cost that keeps climbing, a promise already made.

The big hire and the little hires

The big hire: the moment someone switches to this and leaves the old way behind — what has to be true for them to make that switch.

The little hires: every ordinary time they reach for it afterwards. Say what each of those moments has to feel like for them to keep reaching.
